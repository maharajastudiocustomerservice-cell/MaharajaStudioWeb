Choreography, physics, and FX capabilities

Built for teams replacing fragmented Animator controllers and hand-tuned event timers

Multi-actor choreography & duets

Direct synchronized 2, 3+ character choreography (dance duets, grapples, shared lifts, combat finishers) with frame-accurate timeline synchronization. Mix Humanoid and Generic creature/robot rigs in one sequence.

Dynamic summons & lifecycles

Author ephemeral summons (weapons, familiars, dragons, or avatars) with timeline lifecycle nodes (SpawnActor / DestroyActor), bone attachments, and pooled recycling.

Dynamic motion warping

Pre-interaction approach locomotion automatically bridges starting distances, scaling movement speed and adapting to terrain normals so characters reach sync spots without foot sliding.

8 frame-accurate FX channels & decals

Trigger pooled audio, particle systems, surface decals, VFX Graph, transform splines, spawns, camera shakes, and typed C# callbacks on the shared timeline.

Cross-actor prop & FX handover

Transfer weapons, torches, and spell effects between characters with phase-aware Scene View gizmos, floating HUD tools, and real-time auto-keying.

Post-export interaction overrides

Adjust character body spacing, swap clips, and tune alignment parameters without re-exporting FBX animations or modifying C# scripts.

Zero-allocation runtime PlayableGraph

Play sequences in one line (MSMotionInteraction.Play) with on-demand mixer layers, dynamic avatar rebinding, and pre-warmed actor and FX pools.

Built-in clip & animation editor

Extract locked FBX animations into editable .anim assets in one click, crop ranges, smooth curves (Median/Gaussian), prune redundant keys, and auto-key live.
