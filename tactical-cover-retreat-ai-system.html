Give agents spatial tactical awareness

Instead of hardcoded waypoints, agents scan nearby geometry via Providers, score candidate spots against 30+ weighted Scorers (visibility, path safety, crossfire angles, threat memory, weapon sweetspots), and follow validated NavMesh routes.

Version 3.0 connects cover selection directly to combat execution—recommending crouch or stand stances, safe corner peeking, obstacle vaulting, coordinated fireteam flanking, and one-click scene diagnostics.
