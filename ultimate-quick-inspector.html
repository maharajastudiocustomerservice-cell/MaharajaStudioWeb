One-click access to everything you iterate on

Ultimate Quick Inspector keeps your frequently edited scene objects, prefabs, materials, and C# scripts pinned inside organized pages so you stop scrolling up and down the Hierarchy and Project folders.

All pages save into a single ScriptableObject asset that plays cleanly with Git and team version control, backed by a lightweight GuidComponent for persistent scene object tracking.

Ultimate Quick Inspector · Editor Window Persistent across sessions

Focused editor productivity

Lightweight by design for solo developers, level designers, artists, and teams

Pin any asset or object

Keep prefabs, scripts, materials, and scene GameObjects one click away without hunting through Project folders or Hierarchy trees.

Multi-page organization & search

Create unlimited custom pages such as "UI Elements", "Player Systems", or "Level Tools" and filter items instantly with the built-in search bar.

Drag-and-drop simplicity

Drop items straight from the Project or Hierarchy windows into your active page with zero boilerplate configuration required.

Robust GUID scene tracking

Maintains reliable references to scene GameObjects via a lightweight GuidComponent even after renames or disabled states, stored cleanly in one ScriptableObject.
