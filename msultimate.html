Architectural vision

Modular by design with zero runtime overhead

Built from the ground up on Unity's UI Toolkit (with IMGUI fallback), MSUltimate eliminates per-frame layout recalculations, keeps inspector scrolling responsive at 120 FPS+, and introduces zero garbage collection or build bloat to standalone player builds.

Whether you are an indie developer shipping your first title, a specialist fixing hierarchy workflows, or a commercial team requiring full uncompiled C# source code, the suite scales cleanly across three licensing paths.

Unity 6 native & pipeline agnostic

Compatible with URP, HDRP, and Built-in Render Pipelines. All editor tooling is isolated from player builds automatically.
