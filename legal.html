Support Boundaries FAQ

Q: The AI is giving me an error about 'Invalid API Key'. Can you fix it?

A: No. This is an issue between you and your AI provider. Please check your dashboard on their website to ensure your key is active and has sufficient credits.

Q: The Tool UI is flickering or the 'Generate' button isn't clicking. Can you fix it?

A: Yes! This is a software bug in our tool. Please report this to us so we can issue a patch promptly.

Q: The AI generated a ScriptableObject that broke my game. Will you fix the data?

A: No. AI output is probabilistic. Always backup your project and manually verify what the AI generates before saving.
